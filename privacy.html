Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

Soungui Fitness ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Soungui Member Portal mobile and web application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

When you register for and use the Soungui Member Portal, we collect the following information to provide our fitness and coaching services:

  • Account Information: Your full name, email address, username, and an encrypted (SHA-256 hashed) version of your password.
  • Scheduling Data: Group class reservations and individual gym session bookings.
  • Fitness & Nutrition Data: Daily meal logs, calorie entries, workout routines, and fitness assessments linked to your profile.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data strictly to:

  • Authenticate your member account and display your personalized training and nutrition plans.
  • Schedule and manage your gym sessions and group classes on our training calendar.
  • Sync your daily nutrition and session history across your devices.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your account and training logs are securely stored using Google Workspace services (Google Sheets, Google Drive, and Google Calendar). Passwords are encrypted using SHA-256 hashing before storage.

4. Account & Data Deletion

You may delete your account and associated profile records at any time directly inside the app by tapping the Delete Account button at the bottom of your member dashboard. You may also request manual data removal by contacting us directly.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us at: [soungui@sounguifitness.com]
